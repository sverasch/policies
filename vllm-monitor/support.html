vLLM Monitor — Support

Last updated: 2026-10-22

vLLM Monitor is an unofficial menu bar app for monitoring vLLM inference servers through their Prometheus /metrics endpoints. It collects nothing — see the privacy policy.

Bug reports & questions

Email vllmmonitor@citadelofricks.ai. As a reply is faster with context, please include:

  • the app version (shown in the About window) and your macOS version;
  • whether the problem happens with a real server or the built-in demo instance (Settings → "Add Demo Instance" reproduces most UI issues with no server at all);
  • for stats problems: your vLLM version and a short excerpt from your server's /metrics page (redact anything sensitive);
  • for History/CSV problems: an exported CSV of the affected range, if you can produce one.

Quick troubleshooting

  • No stats appear — confirm the endpoint responds: curl http://your-server:8000/metrics. The app is sandboxed and follows Apple's network rules: plain HTTP works for localhost only; remote servers need HTTPS with a valid certificate.
  • Stats look wrong for your server — not every vLLM build exposes every metric; missing metrics show as empty rather than zero.
