vLLM Monitor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22

vLLM Monitor is a menu bar utility that displays live metrics from vLLM inference servers. It collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking of any kind. This matches the app's "Data Not Collected" privacy label on the App Store.

What leaves your Mac

Nothing, except one thing you control directly: the app sends periodic HTTP(S) GET requests to the Prometheus /metrics endpoint URLs you configure, and reads the metrics they return. Those requests contain no identifiers about you or your device; they are exactly the requests any Prometheus-compatible client would make. The app contacts no other server.

What is stored on your Mac

All app data stays on your Mac, inside the app's sandboxed container under ~/Library/Application Support/vLLMMonitor/:

  • your configured server instances and display preferences;
  • a local SQLite database of metric history used for the History window charts and CSV exports;
  • lifetime counter totals (a small JSON file).

This data is never uploaded, synced, or shared. Deleting the app removes it.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs or services. vLLM Monitor is an unofficial, independent application: it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the vLLM project. "vLLM" is a trademark of the vLLM project.
